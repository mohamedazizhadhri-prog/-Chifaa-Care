# Quick Reference: Doctor Calling Feature

## 🎯 Location
**Chat Header** - Top right corner of any doctor conversation

## 🔘 Buttons

| Button | Icon | Color | Action |
|--------|------|-------|--------|
| Voice Call | 📞 | Green | Audio only call |
| Video Call | 📹 | Blue | Video + audio call |

## 📊 Status Indicators

| Status | Badge | Color | Meaning |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|
| Idle | None | - | No active call |
| Calling... | 📞 | Yellow | Waiting for answer |
| In Call | 📞 | Green | Active call |

## 🎬 Quick Actions

### Make a Call
1. Click green 📞 (voice) or blue 📹 (video)
2. Wait for answer
3. Start talking!

### Answer a Call
1. Banner appears at top
2. Click "Accept" ✅ or "Decline" ❌
3. Call begins (if accepted)

### End a Call
Click the red "End Call" button in the call panel

## ⚡ Keyboard Shortcuts
None currently (future enhancement)

## ✅ Requirements
- ✅ Both doctors online
- ✅ Modern browser
- ✅ Microphone (all calls)
- ✅ Camera (video calls)
- ✅ Good internet

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Buttons disabled | Refresh page or end current call |
| No audio | Check mic permissions |
| No video | Check camera permissions |
| Can't connect | Both doctors must be online |

## 📱 Browser Support
✅ Chrome | ✅ Firefox | ✅ Safari | ✅ Edge | ❌ IE

## 🔐 Permissions
**First call will prompt for:**
- 🎤 Microphone access
- 📹 Camera access (video only)

Click "Allow" to enable calling!
